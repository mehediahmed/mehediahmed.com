Leave No Witnesses

A first-person stealth-action game for PC, in development. Guards hunt you by sight and sound, and anything you leave behind can give you away.

What I built:

  • A custom URP renderer for the pixel-art look. It captures the scene into small cubemaps, keeps only the visible texels in a compute pass and draws each one as a quad in the world, so pixels hold their place in 3D as the camera moves. GPU-driven, with nothing read back to the CPU each frame.
  • Guard perception: sight and sound checks for 100 NPCs inside a 1 ms frame budget, in Burst-compiled C# on the Job System.
  • Injuries instead of a health bar: pain, blood loss, vitality and damage per body zone. Characters who aren't hurt cost nothing per frame.
  • A semantic octree the AI queries for cover, sightlines and blood evidence without walking the scene graph every frame.
  • Hard walls between AI, injuries and rendering: they talk only through shared interfaces and ids, enforced by a test suite.
