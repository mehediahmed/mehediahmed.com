Leave No Witnesses

A first-person stealth-action game for PC, still in development. Guards hunt you by sight and sound, and anything you leave behind can give you away.

What I built for it:

  • A custom URP renderer for the game's pixel-art look. It captures the scene into small cubemaps, keeps only the visible texels in a compute pass, and draws each one as a quad in the world, so the pixels hold their place in 3D as the camera moves instead of sitting flat on the screen. It's GPU-driven: no per-frame counts come back to the CPU.
  • Guard perception: sight and sound checks for 100 NPCs inside a 1 ms frame budget, in Burst-compiled C# on the Job System.
  • Injuries instead of a health bar. Each character tracks pain, blood loss, vitality and damage to each body zone, so a hit can kill, knock someone out or leave them bleeding out, depending on the weapon and where it lands. Characters who aren't hurt cost nothing per frame.
  • A semantic octree the AI uses to reason about cover, sightlines and blood evidence without walking the scene graph every frame.
  • Hard walls between systems. AI, injuries and rendering talk only through shared interfaces and ids, and a test suite fails if any one of them reaches into another.
